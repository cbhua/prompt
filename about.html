Hello there! 👋

This is a collection of prompts for learning, scientific papers, and setting up a new computer, recorded here for easy reuse.

How to use?

  1. Find the corresponding prompt page based on your needs
  2. Fill in paper forms, or read and copy a computer setup prompt
  3. Click the copy button at the bottom of the page
  4. Find a LLM or anything looks like a LLM you can access, send to it

Enjoy!
